## zh-CN

在同一页面比较 Default、Mono 和 Material 的亮暗外观。六个预览分别使用独立主题作用域，输入、开关和按钮均可操作；调整站点主题不会改变各预览的固定外观。

Material 使用品牌色 **#0073E6**。如需在应用中引入、自定义或嵌套主题，请阅读[主题使用与定制](/guide/theming)。

## en-US

Compare Default, Mono and Material in light and dark appearances. All six previews use independent theme scopes with working inputs, switches and buttons. Changing the site theme keeps each preview's explicit appearance.

Material uses **#0073E6** as its brand color. See [Theming](/guide/theming) to import, customize or nest themes in an application.
