## zh-CN

正delay延迟显示以减少闪烁；关闭立即生效。配置变化取消旧任务并重新计时，卸载清理定时器。无正delay时随Vue更新显示。

## en-US

A positive delay prevents loading flashes; closing is immediate. Configuration changes cancel and restart pending work, and unmount clears timers. Without a positive delay, loading follows the Vue update.
