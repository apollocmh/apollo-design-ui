## zh-CN

动态切换遮罩blur、className、style及false；同步演示当前scaleStep和getContainer。打开后可用工具栏按钮切换，保持同一预览与变换。显式容器优先于Provider，移除覆盖恢复默认容器。

## en-US

Switch mask blur/className/style or hide it, with live scaleStep and getContainer. The toolbar updates the open preview without resetting transforms. An explicit container overrides the Provider; removing it restores the default.
